Gmail Multi Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Gmail Multi is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own Mac. It connects the owner's Google accounts to the Claude desktop app. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen, Gmail Multi can read, send, draft, label and trash email in Gmail, and read and create files in Google Drive, Sheets and Docs.

Where data goes

  • Gmail Multi runs entirely on the owner's computer. There is no Gmail Multi server, and the developer receives no data.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer.
  • When the owner asks Claude to work with their email or files, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude service to answer that request, under Anthropic's own privacy terms.
  • Google data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own requests.

Limited Use

Gmail Multi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the Gmail Multi folder from the computer removes all stored sign-in tokens.
